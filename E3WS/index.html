What is E3WS?

E3WS is the core AI algorithm behind rapid earthquake early warning from the earliest available seismic signal.

The method uses the first 3 seconds of seismic records to detect earthquakes, identify the P-wave phase, and estimate source parameters such as magnitude and location.

Its purpose is speed with scientific reliability: converting the earliest ground-motion information into warning decisions before damaging waves arrive.

The System

E3WS combines real-time seismic acquisition, machine learning, and rapid source characterization.

Data Capture

High-cost and low-cost sensors continuously record seismic signals and provide the first seconds of motion.

Machine Learning

E3WS processes the first 3 seconds of records to detect the event and estimate magnitude and location.

Warning Decision

The output can support alert workflows, institutional protocols, and public-safety decisions.
